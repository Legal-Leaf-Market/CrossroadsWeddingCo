# Graphics brief: the DJ business platform (working name "Crossroads Agency OS")

Hand this whole file to a design chat. It has everything needed to make on-brand,
truthful marketing graphics. Written 2026-11-11 from decisions Jake Kennedy (CEO) made.

## 1. What we are selling

One app that runs a DJ business from first inquiry to last dance, **with the books
built in**. It replaces the planning app, the CRM, the contract tool, and
QuickBooks.

- **Price:** $50 a month, flat. Every feature. One plan.
- **Trial:** 14 days free, no card.
- **Free for your clients:** couples never pay or download anything.
- **Who it's for first:** wedding and event DJs (solo and multi-op). Next: planners,
  photographers, venues.

## 2. The three messages (use these, in this order of priority)

1. **Your books are built in. No QuickBooks.**
   Bank and card feeds, transactions sorted into categories, profit and loss, and a
   ledger ready at tax time, in the same app as your bookings. A DJ on QuickBooks
   Simple Start pays about $38 a month for that part alone (QuickBooks' published list
   price, November 2026).
2. **A real accountant on your side.** Jake Kennedy, CEO, is a seasoned accountant,
   technologist and DJ. He runs his own DJ company (Crossroads Wedding and Event Co.)
   on this software. Tax filing is available through Smart CFO, under confidentiality.
   Jake's line, quote it exactly: **"You don't need anyone but me."**
3. **The night runs itself.** A live timeline: when toasts run long, every later time
   shifts on every phone that has it open, including the venue coordinator's and the
   photographer's.

## 4. What NOT to put on a graphic

- **No made-up numbers.** No user counts, "trusted by", star ratings, revenue, time
  saved, or percentages unless Jake gives the real figure.
- **No testimonials** unless a real customer wrote them and agreed to be quoted.
  No stock-photo "customers".
- **No competitor claims without a source.** If you compare to EverSet, Vibo, HoneyBook,
  DJ Event Planner or QuickBooks, use only their own published prices and say "as of
  November 2026". Never say a competitor lacks something unless their own site says so.
- **Don't say "CPA"** for Jake. He is an accountant. Don't promise a tax refund,
  savings amount or audit protection.
- **Don't say "free"** about the platform except "14 days free" and "free for your clients".
- **No em dashes** anywhere in the copy (house rule). Use a period, comma or colon.
- **Couples' photos and names:** only from events where the couple agreed, and never
  photos that center on children. Crossroads' own event photos are on
  crossroadsweddingco.shop/weddings/... and may be used.

## 5. Look and feel

- **Mood:** confident, warm, a little rock-and-roll. The person behind it is a working DJ
  who is also an accountant: one foot in the booth, one in the ledger.
- **Palette (exact values from the live product's stylesheet):** cream background
  `#FAF5EC`, charcoal `#2B2622`, ink (body text) `#3D362F`, terracotta accent `#C1633D`
  (darker `#A34D2B` for text on light), parchment `#F1E6D3`, gold label `#CF9D4C`.
  For dark graphics: charcoal ground with cream type and a terracotta highlight.
- **Type:** Spectral (display, serif) for headlines, Karla (sans) for body. Both are
  free on Google Fonts.
- **Imagery:** real DJ booths, decks, dance floors and phones showing the app. Real
  event rooms, not stock ballrooms.
- **Motif ideas:** a ledger line that turns into a waveform; a run-sheet time that
  slides forward; "$50" set big like a price tag on gear.

## 7. In person (guerrilla) pieces

Every printed piece carries its own QR code with a tag, so the dashboard's "Platform
signups" panel shows which ones work. The link shape is
`crossroadsweddingco.shop/signup?src=<tag>`: lowercase letters, numbers and dashes, one
tag per piece and place, and never reuse a tag for something else once it's printed.

| Piece | Where | Tag example |
|---|---|---|
| Pocket card (3.5x2) with QR | DJ meetups, gear shops, every gig you play | `card-jake` |
| Expo table tent and flyer | Bridal and DJ expos | `expo-feb` |
| Laptop / DJ booth sticker | On Jake's own rig at every event | `booth` |
| Gear-shop counter card | Guitar Center, local pro audio | `shop-<store>` |
| Venue one-pager | Venue walk-ins (for Crossroads' DJ service, `/venues?src=venue`) | `venue` |
