## 1. Which plan

| | GigSalad | The Bash |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0, 5% booking fee, fewest leads | No free tier found |
| Paid | Pro about $139 / 3 months (2.5% fee); Featured about $169 / 3 months (2.5% fee, highest visibility) | Basic about $79–$129 / year (leads within 100 miles, up to 4 services); Pro about $159–$219 / year (up to 6 services) |

Prices come from help pages and third-party reviews and vary by source. Confirm on each site's
join page before paying.

**Recommendation:** GigSalad **Featured, 3 months** (December is the busiest party month, and
visibility is the point), and The Bash **Pro** (6 services means DJ, sound, MC, acoustic, bar,
corporate entertainment). Judge both in February on bookings, the same rule as every paid channel.

Two things to know:
- **The Bash is owned by The Knot Worldwide**, the same company as WeddingPro. Memberships
  **auto-renew** unless you opt out within 30 days of expiration. Put a calendar reminder in now.
- On GigSalad, mark gigs as booked through the site. Free members are required to; paid members
  should anyway, because booked counts lift your ranking.

## 8. FAQ answers

- **What's included in the $1,500 corporate rate?** DJ, sound system, wireless mic for speeches and awards, and MC.
- **How far do you travel?** About two hours from Columbus. Past about an hour there's a $100–$150 travel fee, quoted up front.
- **Do you take requests?** Yes, plus a do-not-play list.
- **Do you supply alcohol?** No. We staff and pour; the host or venue provides it.
- **How do we book?** Tell us the date and venue. We confirm within 24 hours, and a deposit locks the date.
- **Can you do short notice?** Usually, yes. Ask us.
