# Asset brief: floral frame for Kat & Tanis, November 7 2026

Paste this whole file into ChatGPT (image generation). Ask for the assets one at a
time, in the order below, and save each with the exact filename given.

## What this is for

A guest-facing "Order of Events" web page, dark, portrait, read mostly on phones.
The couple's own invitation suite is near-black with purple and lilac watercolor
florals running up the left and right edges, and we want the page to feel like it
came from the same set. The art is decoration only. It sits behind and beside the
text and must never compete with it.

## House rules for every asset

- **No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks.** All wording is set
  in the page itself.
- **PNG with a real transparent background** is strongly preferred. If transparency
  is not available, deliver on **pure black `#000000`** and say so, because we can
  blend pure black out in the browser. Any other background colour is unusable.
- **Watercolor illustration**, soft edges, visible paper texture, hand-painted feel.
  Not photographic, not 3D, not vector-flat, not AI-glossy.
- **Palette:** deep violet, lilac, lavender, dusty periwinkle, with white and
  cream daisy petals and small dark navy-purple berries. Muted, slightly desaturated,
  as if painted on cold-press paper. No pink, no red, no warm gold.
- **Composition must hug one edge** and fade to nothing toward the middle of the
  frame. The centre is where text goes.
- Deliver at the pixel dimensions given. Larger is fine, smaller is not.

## Checks before sending them on

- Open each PNG over a black background. If a grey or white box appears behind the
  art, the transparency did not survive; ask for it again.
- Each file under about 1.5 MB. If bigger, ask for the same image at 70 percent scale.
- Look at the middle of each column: it should be empty enough to lay a line of text
  across without the text touching a petal.
