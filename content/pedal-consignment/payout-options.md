# Pedal consignment: the three payout options

Rough draft, 2026-09-05. Numbers are 60 / 80 / 90 per Jacob. Beat it up.

## Short message (send this one)

Three ways to do this. Pick whichever fits.

**1. Cash up front, zero work for you.**
We pay 60% of market value today, which is in line with most shops. You ship the pedals to us and you're done.

**2. Half now, the rest as they sell.**
You get 80% of market value after Reverb fees. We pay half of that the day we agree, and the pedals stay at your place. We handle the listing, the marketing, and the customer service. When one sells, we email you a prepaid label. You wrap the pedal in bubble wrap, put it in a free USPS flat rate box, stick the label on, and drop it anywhere that takes packages. The post office doesn't even have to be open. The rest gets paid out as each one sells.

**3. Max payout.**
Same seat as option 2, but you get 90% of market value after Reverb fees. Nothing up front. You get paid as quickly as the money comes in, one pedal at a time.

Less up front means more overall. Your call.

## Even tighter (text message length)

Three options on the pedals:

1. 60% of market value, cash today. Ship them to us, done.
2. 80% after Reverb fees. Half now, pedals stay with you, we list and sell them, you drop each one in a free USPS box with our prepaid label when it sells. Rest paid as they go.
3. 90% after Reverb fees. Same as option 2 with nothing up front. Paid as each one sells.

Less up front, more overall. Your call.

## Notes from the read-through

- Option 1 says "in line with most shops." Not "or better."
- Options 2 and 3 share one structure on purpose: less money up front, more money overall. The only things that change are the percentage and the up-front half.
- Drop-off wording: any location that accepts packages, does not have to be an open post office. That is the line that makes option 2 and 3 feel painless, keep it.
- No em dashes anywhere in the sent copy.
