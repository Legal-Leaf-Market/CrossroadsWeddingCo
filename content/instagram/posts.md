# Instagram posts — the $1,000 day rate

Four single-image posts (1080×1080) explaining the flat day rate, ending on the
intro call. Images are in this folder; regenerate them with `pnpm ig:posts`
after editing `scripts/generate-ig-posts.mjs`.

They're written to run in order over a week or two — 1 and 2 do the explaining,
3 handles the "what's the catch" question, 4 asks for the call. Each one also
stands alone if you'd rather space them out.

**Before posting:** these were designed against the website's palette and
typefaces (charcoal / cream / terracotta, Spectral + Karla), not against your
existing grid — I couldn't load the Instagram account from here. If the grid has
drifted somewhere else, tell me and I'll re-cut them.

## Notes

- **Hashtags are all national.** Once the service area is settled, swap three or
  four of them for local tags — those are what actually reach couples who can
  book you.
- **"Link in bio"** assumes the bio points at crossroadsweddingco.com. All four
  captions now ask for the 30-minute call, so once the booking page is live,
  pointing the bio link straight at it will convert better than the homepage.
- Every claim here comes off the website — the rate, what's included, bar and
  acoustic being quoted separately, the call length. Nothing invented. If any of
  it is wrong, the website is wrong too and both should change together.
