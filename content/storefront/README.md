# WeddingPro storefront slides

Seven branded slides for The Knot / WeddingWire storefront photo gallery
(minimum 5 photos, at least 2000px on the longest side, max 10MB each).
These render at 2160x2160 PNG; regenerate with `pnpm storefront:slides`
after editing `scripts/generate-storefront-slides.mjs`.

They are original Crossroads graphics (site palette, Spectral + Karla),
not photos, so the marketplace's "you own or have rights to these images"
confirmation is honestly checkable.

## Upload order

The first photo becomes the storefront cover, so keep 01 first.

1. `01-the-rate.png`: $1,000 flat rate, matches the storefront headline
2. `02-whats-included.png`: what the rate covers
3. `03-no-packages.png`: no tiers, no upsells
4. `04-acoustic.png`: live solo acoustic add-on, flat $500
5. `05-bar-service.png`: bar service, $500 minimum, quoted on the call
6. `06-planning-hub.png`: the planning hub, benefit-first
7. `07-book-a-call.png`: the 30-minute call and the service area

## When real photos exist

Marketplaces rank storefronts with real event photos above graphics, and
couples trust them more. As soon as there are shots from actual gigs
(setup, acoustic set, packed dance floor), swap them in ahead of these
slides and let the graphics fill the tail of the gallery instead of the
front.
